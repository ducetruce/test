# OpenRing — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 11 September 2026_

OpenRing is a personal, open-source iOS application. It is not a service, and there is no
company, server or organisation behind it.

## What data the app handles

With your permission, OpenRing reads your own health data from the Oura API: sleep periods,
daily activity, readiness, blood oxygen, daytime stress, workouts, and basic profile
information such as your age and email address.

## Where that data goes

Nowhere except your device.

- All data is stored **locally on your iPhone**, in the app's private container.
- OAuth credentials (client id, client secret, access and refresh tokens) are stored in the
  **iOS Keychain**.
- The app makes network requests to exactly two hosts, both operated by Oura:
  `cloud.ouraring.com` (to sign in) and `api.ouraring.com` (to read your data).
- There is **no OpenRing server**. No data is transmitted to the developer or to any third
  party.
- There is **no analytics, telemetry, advertising, tracking or crash reporting** of any kind.

## Who can see your data

Only you. The developer of OpenRing has no ability to access, receive or view any data
handled by the app.

## Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you remove it. You can erase the local copy at any time from
Settings, which does not affect your Oura account. Deleting the app removes all locally
stored data and Keychain entries.

You can revoke OpenRing's access to your Oura account at any time from your Oura account
settings, independently of this app.

## Children

OpenRing is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the project's repository.
